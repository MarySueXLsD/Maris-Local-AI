## New

- **Ask Maris about Maris.** "How do I connect Claude?", "can I use you from my phone?", "which models do I have?": Maris now reads the app's own Help and looks at what is set up on your computer, then answers with the steps. Before, a model could answer from what it believed about AI assistants in general, for example that Maris cannot be connected to Claude at all (it can: **Models → API**).
- **Page names in answers open the page.** When an answer or a Help article names a page such as **Models → API** or **Settings → Phone**, a click on the name opens it.
- **Help**: a new page, *Maris on your phone*, on getting the Android app, pairing it and what the phone may do.

## Changed

- In low-power mode a question about the app brings the help along, and only the part of an article that answers the question is read, so a small model on a slow computer answers it too.
- The `maris` terminal command shows the names of pages and Help articles in bold instead of as links it cannot open.
